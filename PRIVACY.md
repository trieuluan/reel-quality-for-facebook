# Privacy Policy

_Reel Quality for Facebook. Last updated: October 15, 2026._

Reel Quality for Facebook does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

- **No data collection.** The extension has no analytics, tracking, accounts or remote servers.
- **What it reads.** On facebook.com pages, the extension reads the video manifests that Facebook already sends to your browser. It needs them to list the available video qualities. This information is processed in memory, in the page, and is never sent anywhere.
- **Network requests.** The extension only downloads video segments from Facebook's own video servers (`*.fbcdn.net`), and only for the quality you selected.
- **Support link.** The ☕ Support link in the menu opens ko-fi.com in a new tab, and only when you click it. The MoMo/VietQR code shown to Vietnamese users is built into the extension, so showing it makes no network request.
- **Local storage.** Two settings are saved in your browser's local storage for facebook.com: the quality you chose and the button position. They never leave your device. Clearing site data for facebook.com removes them.
- **Permissions.** The extension requests no extra browser permissions. It only runs on `www.facebook.com` and `web.facebook.com`.

Questions: please [open an issue](https://github.com/trieuluan/reel-quality-for-facebook/issues).
